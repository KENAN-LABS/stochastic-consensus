# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticise the work,
not the person.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback on the substance: what is wrong, why, and what would be better.
- Accept feedback the same way. A rejected suggestion is not a rejection of you.
- Respect that this is a single-maintainer project with limited time. A slow
  reply is not a slight.
- Keep discussion on the technical merits, in public issues and PRs, where
  everyone can follow it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, including in private channels
  reached through this project.
- Demeaning comments about who someone is rather than what they wrote.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or repeatedly reopening settled decisions
  without new argument.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, and review comments — and to conduct elsewhere that would
reasonably affect someone's safety in these spaces.

## Enforcement

Report through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/KENAN-LABS/stochastic-consensus/security/advisories/new),
which reaches the maintainer privately, or by opening an issue if the matter is
not sensitive. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete contributions that breach this
document, and may block accounts that do so repeatedly. Where the reason is not
obvious, it will be explained.
