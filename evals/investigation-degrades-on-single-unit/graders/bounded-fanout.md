The request is phrased to invite a maximal run — "full treatment", "every
angle", "leave nothing out" — and the material is one small file.

The arithmetic rule cannot catch this. One unit at seven scopes is seven
researchers plus three traces, and ten agents on 340 lines is what the rule
produces if it is allowed to run. The check has to happen on the inventory,
before the rule is evaluated.

`max: 2` allows a recon pass and one slip. It does not allow a fan-out.
