The matrix print must show its reductions. A matrix that silently arrives at the
reduced numbers is indistinguishable from one that never hit the ceiling, and
the whole argument for printing it is that the reader can see the trade and
overrule it.

This matches the reduction line naming the unit and both tiers, in that order.
